Care By Plants

General Tips

Good to Know

01

Water Wisely

Most indoor plants prefer slightly dry soil before watering. Always check the soil moisture before adding water.

02

Give Proper Sunlight

Place your plants where they receive enough indirect sunlight without exposing them to harsh afternoon sun.

03

Maintain Humidity

Tropical plants grow better with higher humidity. Use a humidifier or place plants together.

04

Check for Pests

Inspect leaves regularly for insects and remove damaged leaves to keep plants healthy.
