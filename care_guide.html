CARE GUIDE

Keep your plants happy

Learn to care for every plant with simple tips for beginners.

Care By Plants

Golden Pothos

  • Light: Bright, indirect sunlight.
  • Water: Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil is dry.
  • Soil: Well-draining indoor potting mix.
  • Temperature: 18–29°C.
  • Humidity: Average indoor humidity.
  • Tip: Trim vines regularly for fuller growth.

Calathea Orbifolia

  • Light: Bright, indirect light.
  • Water: Keep soil slightly moist.
  • Soil: Rich, well-draining soil.
  • Temperature: 18–27°C.
  • Humidity: High humidity (60%+).
  • Tip: Use filtered water to prevent brown leaf edges.

Marble Queen Pothos

  • Light: Bright indirect light.
  • Water: Water when soil feels dry.
  • Soil: Well-draining potting mix.
  • Temperature: 18–29°C.
  • Humidity: Moderate humidity.
  • Tip: More light keeps the white variegation bright.

Red Aglaonema

  • Light: Low to bright indirect light.
  • Water: Water when the top half of the soil is dry.
  • Soil: Loose, well-draining soil.
  • Temperature: 18–30°C.
  • Humidity: Average indoor humidity.
  • Tip: Remove yellow leaves to encourage healthy growth.

Calathea Medallion

  • Light: Bright indirect light.
  • Water: Keep soil evenly moist.
  • Soil: Rich, well-draining soil.
  • Temperature: 18–27°C.
  • Humidity: High humidity.
  • Tip: Avoid direct sunlight to protect leaf colors.

Aglaonema Silver

  • Light: Medium to bright indirect light.
  • Water: Water when the top soil is dry.
  • Soil: Well-draining potting mix.
  • Temperature: 18–30°C.
  • Humidity: Moderate humidity.
  • Tip: Clean leaves regularly to keep them healthy.

Alocasia Elephant Ear

  • Light: Bright indirect light.
  • Water: Keep soil consistently moist.
  • Soil: Moist but well-draining soil.
  • Temperature: 18–30°C.
  • Humidity: High humidity.
  • Tip: Reduce watering during winter dormancy.

Bird of Paradise

  • Light: Bright light with some morning sun.
  • Water: Water when the top soil dries.
  • Soil: Rich, well-draining soil.
  • Temperature: 18–30°C.
  • Humidity: Moderate humidity.
  • Tip: Rotate the pot for even growth.

Monstera Borsigiana

  • Light: Bright indirect light.
  • Water: Water when the top soil is dry.
  • Soil: Chunky, well-draining soil.
  • Temperature: 18–30°C.
  • Humidity: Moderate to high humidity.
  • Tip: Use a moss pole for better growth.

Fiddle Leaf Fig (Large)

  • Light: Bright indirect light.
  • Water: Water when the top 5 cm of soil is dry.
  • Soil: Well-draining soil.
  • Temperature: 18–27°C.
  • Humidity: Moderate humidity.
  • Tip: Rotate weekly and wipe leaves clean.

Ficus Elastica (Rubber Plant)

  • Light: Bright indirect light.
  • Water: Water when the top soil feels dry.
  • Soil: Well-draining potting mix.
  • Temperature: 18–29°C.
  • Humidity: Average indoor humidity.
  • Tip: Avoid overwatering and dust the leaves regularly.

Calathea Velvet

  • Light: Bright, indirect sunlight.
  • Water: Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil is dry.
  • Soil: Well-draining indoor potting mix.
  • Temperature: 18–29°C.
  • Humidity: Average indoor humidity.
  • Tip: Trim vines regularly for fuller growth.
General Tips

Good to Know

01

Water Wisely

Most indoor plants prefer slightly dry soil before watering. Always check the soil moisture before adding water.

02

Give Proper Sunlight

Place your plants where they receive enough indirect sunlight without exposing them to harsh afternoon sun.

03

Maintain Humidity

Tropical plants grow better with higher humidity. Use a humidifier or place plants together.

04

Check for Pests

Inspect leaves regularly for insects and remove damaged leaves to keep plants healthy.
