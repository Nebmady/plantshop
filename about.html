Family Founded,
future forward.

ROKKHA began as a small family business with a simple dream: to bring people closer to nature through beautiful, healthy plants. What started as a passion for gardening has grown into a trusted destination for plant lovers looking to create greener, happier homes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make plant care simple, enjoyable, and accessible for everyone. We provide healthy plants, premium gardening products, and expert guidance to help every customer grow with confidence.

At ROKKHA, we are committed to sustainability, quality, and excellent customer service. We aim to inspire people to create beautiful green spaces while protecting the environment for future generations.

Our History

1997
ROKKHA was founded as a small family-owned nursery with a passion for growing healthy plants. We started by serving our local community with carefully selected indoor and outdoor plants, earning trust through quality and personalized service.
2004
As our community expanded, so did our collection. We introduced a wider variety of houseplants, decorative pots, and gardening accessories while continuing to provide expert plant care advice to every customer.
2020
To make plant shopping easier, ROKKHA launched its online platform. Customers could browse our collections, learn through plant care guides, and order plants directly from home, making greenery more accessible than ever.
NOW & THE FUTURE
Today, ROKKHA continues to grow with a focus on sustainability, innovation, and customer satisfaction. We remain dedicated to helping people create healthier, greener living spaces while building a community that shares a love for plants and nature.
